# RentRecord — interview plan

## Research status

No completed interviews or participant findings have been documented. The following is a plan and recording template.

## Goal

Check whether target tenants need a structured place for household bills, apartment records and moving tasks, and whether local benefits add value.

## Participants

Recruit approximately five tenants from the intended audience: students, young adults and international tenants in Finland. Collect minimal anonymous notes with permission.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last household bill you needed to find. Where was it?
2. How do you check what is due this month? Show the steps without revealing private details.
3. Tell me about your last move. How did you keep apartment photos and moving tasks?
4. Have you needed to retrieve an old condition photo or follow up a deposit? What happened?
5. After seeing the concept, which part would you use? Which part would you ignore?
6. Would manually adding bills be worthwhile? What would make you stop?
7. What makes a local offer useful: location, discount, category or convenience?

Avoid asking only “Do you like the app?” and avoid claiming that a participant's interest proves demand.

## Partner questions

Ask prospective local businesses what benefit they could offer, what restrictions apply, how redemption would work and what reporting they require. No business is a confirmed partner until an agreement exists.
